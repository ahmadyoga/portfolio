# Quality Bar

Before considering the portfolio complete, evaluate it as:

## Recruiter

Can I understand who Ahmad is within 10–20 seconds?

## Hiring Manager

Can I understand his engineering ability within 2–5 minutes?

## Senior Engineer

Does the technical presentation feel credible?

## Designer

Does the motion feel intentional?

## Mobile User

Does the site remain fast and usable?

## Performance Engineer

Are animations and assets reasonably optimized?

## Final Impression

The website should make the viewer think:

> "I want to interview this engineer."

The portfolio should impress because it is exceptionally well-designed and technically thoughtful, not because it contains the maximum number of effects.
