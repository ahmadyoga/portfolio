# Objectives

## Primary Objectives

1. Establish Ahmad as a credible Mobile Software Engineer.
2. Showcase a small number of strong projects rather than a large project dump.
3. Demonstrate engineering depth through case studies.
4. Make the portfolio feel alive through purposeful animation and interaction.
5. Make the experience memorable while remaining professional.
6. Complement the one-page ATS resume rather than duplicating it.
7. Make contacting Ahmad extremely easy.

## Recruiter Objective

A recruiter should understand within approximately 10–20 seconds:

- who Ahmad is
- what he specializes in
- what he has built
- where to inspect his work

## Hiring Manager Objective

Within 2–5 minutes, a hiring manager should be able to understand:

- Ahmad's technical scope
- his project experience
- how he approaches engineering
- the complexity of his work
- his ability to explain technical decisions

## Senior Engineer Objective

A senior engineer reviewing the portfolio should find the technical presentation:

- credible
- concrete
- appropriately detailed
- free of exaggerated claims
- focused on actual engineering decisions

## Design Objective

The site should feel:

- premium
- editorial
- technical
- minimal
- expressive
- intentional

It should not feel like an AI-generated template.

## Critical Principle

The goal is NOT:

> "Use as many animations as possible."

The goal is:

> "Create a portfolio that feels alive because motion supports the story."
